## 1. Niche-by-Niche Pitch Cheatsheets (One-Liners & Proof)

### 🥇 Segment 1: Industrial Manufacturers & Distributors (Fastest Cash)
- **Target Audience**: Factory owners, managing directors, operations heads in auto parts, hosiery, hand tools, textiles, packaging (e.g. Ludhiana, Gujarat, NCR).
- **Their Core Pain**: Orders taken on handwritten slips and unstructured WhatsApp voice notes; inventory stock mismatches; manual Tally reconciliation; dispatch errors.
- **The Hook (WhatsApp / Call)**:
  > *"Hi [Name], saw you run [Company]. Are you still managing dealer dispatches on WhatsApp chats and handwritten slips? We built a 1-tap ordering app for Garg Enterprises in Ludhiana that dropped their dispatch mistakes from 14% down to zero. Would you be open to a 15-min look at how it works?"*
- **Proof Asset**: [Garg Enterprises Case Study](https://www.digitalbuilders.in/portfolio/garg-enterprises) · [Custom ERP/CRM Page](https://www.digitalbuilders.in/services/erp-crm)
- **Ticket Size**: ₹2,49,000 – ₹5,99,000 (INR)

### 🥈 Segment 2: Retail Stores & D2C Brands
- **Target Audience**: Local retail chains, garment showrooms, D2C brands, regional distributors.
- **Their Core Pain**: High marketplace commissions (Amazon/Swiggy 20-30%); no direct customer data; store closing at 8 PM stops sales.
- **The Hook**:
  > *"Hi [Name], let's get your store taking orders online and directly on WhatsApp with instant UPI checkout—so your catalog keeps selling even when the physical shutters are down. Takes 3 weeks to go live. Want to see a live demo?"*
- **Proof Asset**: [E-Commerce Services](https://www.digitalbuilders.in/#services) · [Interactive Estimator](https://www.digitalbuilders.in/estimator)
- **Ticket Size**: ₹99,000 – ₹2,79,000 (INR)

### 🥈 Segment 3: Clinics, Diagnostic Labs & Telehealth
- **Target Audience**: Specialist doctors, multi-doctor clinics, diagnostic centers, wellness practitioners.

- **The Hook**:

- **Proof Asset**: [GutTalks Case Study](https://www.digitalbuilders.in/portfolio/guttalks)
- **Ticket Size**: ₹99,000 – ₹1,99,000 (INR)

### 🥈 Segment 4: Coaching Institutes & Academies
- **Target Audience**: Institute directors, competitive exam coaching centers, test prep academies.

- **The Hook**:

## 2. The 5-Touch Follow-Up Sequence

Most sales are lost because developers stop after 1 message. Use this exact cadence:

| Touchpoint | Timing | Channel | Script / Message Template |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Touch 1** | Day 0 | WhatsApp / Email | Send initial problem hook + 1-sentence proof + link to case study or 15-min discovery slot. |
| **Touch 2** | Day 2 | WhatsApp | *"Hi [Name], just sharing our 2026 Price Book & Scope Estimator in case you want to ballpark numbers: https://www.digitalbuilders.in/estimator. Have 10 mins this week to review your bottleneck?"* |
| **Touch 3** | Day 5 | WhatsApp / LinkedIn | *"Hi [Name], thought of your workflow — we just published an architecture guide on how manufacturers are eliminating dispatch errors: https://www.digitalbuilders.in/blog/whatsapp-first-erp-crm-indian-manufacturers. Worth a quick read."* |
| **Touch 4** | Day 9 | Call / Voice Note | *"Hi [Name], Ashish here. Dropping a quick note to see if solving [order errors / manual bookings] is still a priority for this quarter, or if you'd like to revisit next month?"* |
| **Touch 5 (Breakup)** | Day 14 | WhatsApp / Email | *"Hi [Name], haven't heard back, so I assume this isn't a priority right now. I'll take a step back so I don't clutter your inbox. If you ever want to streamline [workflow] with a fixed written scope, feel free to reach back out anytime!"* (Often gets a 40%+ reply rate). |

## 3. Objection Handling Battlecards

### Objection 1: *"Your price is too high. Another developer offered ₹25,000."*
- **Response**:
  > *"I completely understand budget is important. The reality of ₹25,000 software is that it's usually built on fragile, bloated plugins with zero automated tests. Six months down the line when it crashes during a peak sale, you end up spending double to rebuild from scratch — 'sasta roye baar-baar'.*
  > 
  > *With us, you get clean, proprietary architecture personally reviewed by a 10+ year Staff Architect, complete IP ownership, and a 30-day warranty. If you want to de-risk, we can start with our ₹19,000 Architecture Discovery Sprint, which is 100% credited to the build contract once we kick off."*

### Objection 2: *"I need to think about it."*
- **Response**:
  > *"Totally fair! Usually when people say they need to think about it, it comes down to one of three things: price, timeline, or trust. Which of those is the biggest open question for you right now?"*

### Objection 3: *"Just send me all details on WhatsApp / Email."*
- **Response**:
  > *"Sending across our PDF Rate Card right now! [Link: digitalbuilders.in/pricing]. Let's jump on a quick 10-minute screen share on Thursday at 3 PM so I can show you the exact screen mockups for your business. Does 3 PM work, or is morning better?"*
