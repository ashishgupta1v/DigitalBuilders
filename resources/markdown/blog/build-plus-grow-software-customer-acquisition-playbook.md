## 1. Why Generic Social Media Marketing Dilutes Software Brands

When most founders think about marketing, they hire a generic agency that charges ₹15,000 to post generic motivational quotes on Instagram. 

This almost never generates high-ticket B2B software leads, SaaS signups, or high-intent client enquiries.

Instead, software customer acquisition requires an **engineering-driven growth framework**:
- **Search Intent Over Fluff**: Capturing buyers actively searching on Google for your exact solution.
- **Sub-Second Speed (Core Web Vitals)**: Ensuring landing pages load in under 500ms so ad traffic doesn't bounce.
- **Zero-Loss Lead Telemetry**: Connecting Meta Conversions API (CAPI) and WhatsApp Webhooks so every single lead is instantly qualified.

### 2. AI Content Engine
Consistent authoritative content without hiring a full-time content team. We use an AI-assisted editorial pipeline (4 to 8 articles/month) reviewed and polished by human technical editors to build search engine authority.

### 3. Run Ads That Bring Real Leads
Targeted Google Search and Meta Performance campaigns. You pay platforms directly for clicks; we engineer high-converting ad copy, negative keyword lists, and server-side tracking.

If a lead enquires at 11:30 PM, they shouldn't wait until 10:00 AM next morning for a reply. Our automated WhatsApp bot answers immediately, qualifies their requirement, and notifies your sales team instantly.
